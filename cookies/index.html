2What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. "Local storage" is similar. They let a site remember your choices and, for analytics, count visits. Some are strictly necessary for the site to work; others are optional and need your consent.

3Your consent

When you first visit, a small banner lets you Accept all, Reject all, or Manage your choice. We use Google Consent Mode: until you accept, analytics are set to "denied" and no analytics cookies are stored. If you accept, analytics cookies are enabled. Strictly necessary storage does not require consent and is always active.

You can withdraw or change your consent at any time. Clearing this site's cookies and site data in your browser removes your saved choice and brings back the consent banner, so you can choose again.
