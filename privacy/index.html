Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BITOQU Signature Estates collects, uses, stores and protects your personal data when you visit bitoqu.com or get in touch with us. We handle your information with the same discretion we bring to every client relationship.

In short

  • We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and we do not build marketing profiles of our visitors.
  • We only collect the information you choose to give us – mainly through the enquiry form or when you message us.
  • We use your details solely to respond to you and arrange a private consultation.
  • We never sell your data. We share it only with the service providers that help us run the site and reply to you.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data at any time by writing to alikhanbitoqu@gmail.com.

2Scope and applicable law

This policy applies to the website at bitoqu.com and to enquiries you send us through it. It describes our practices under the UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (PDPL) and, where you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / UK GDPR).

Once you become a client, the handling of your information is governed by our separate engagement terms, which take precedence over this website policy.

4Browsing the website

Our website does not set advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and loads no tracking scripts. To deliver the site, our hosting and content-delivery providers may briefly process technical request data (such as your IP address) for security and reliability.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and a small animation library from Cloudflare / cdnjs. When these load, those providers may receive your IP address, as is normal for any website that uses them. The site is served over a secure (HTTPS) connection.

5Data stored in your browser

We use one small item of local storage in your browser to remember your chosen language (English, Russian or Turkish), so the site opens in it next time. It stays on your device, is not sent to us or anyone else, and you can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

To show the right country code in the contact form, the site reads your device’s time zone and browser language in your browser only. This is not stored and is not sent to us.

6Contacting us

Channels

You can reach us through the enquiry form (which is delivered to our email inbox by FormSubmit), by booking a call through Calendly, or by email, WhatsApp or Telegram. Each channel is operated by the relevant provider under its own terms; messaging services such as WhatsApp apply their own encryption.

How we use your enquiry

We use the information in your enquiry only to reply to you, understand what you are looking for, and arrange a consultation. We do not use it for advertising and we do not use it to train artificial-intelligence systems.

Sensitive information

Please do not send passport or ID numbers, or payment-card details, through the website form or a first message. We do not need this information to respond to an enquiry.

Information about other people

If you give us personal data about someone else (for example, a family member or co-investor), please make sure they are aware. We use it only for your enquiry and delete it together with your enquiry.

7Who receives your personal data

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only with the service providers that help us operate the website and respond to you, and only to the extent necessary:

  • FormSubmit – delivers the enquiry form to our email inbox.
  • Calendly – lets you book a consultation call.
  • Google – provides our email (Gmail), where enquiries arrive, and Google Fonts, which serves the site’s fonts.
  • Cloudflare / cdnjs – serves a website animation library.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) and Telegram – if you choose to message us through them.
  • Our website hosting provider – stores and serves the site.
  • Professional advisers, and courts or authorities, where we are required to share data by law or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

8International processing

We are based in the United Arab Emirates, and some of our service providers are located in other countries, including the United States and the European Union. When your data is transferred outside your country, we take reasonable steps to ensure it remains protected in line with this policy and applicable law.

10Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or update it;
  • ask us to delete it;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use it;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out;
  • receive a copy of the data you gave us in a portable format; and
  • lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 1. We will respond within the time required by applicable law. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom these rights arise under the GDPR; in the United Arab Emirates, under the PDPL, with oversight by the UAE Data Office.

11Automated decisions

We do not make decisions about you by automated means that would produce a legal or similarly significant effect. The country code shown in the contact form is a convenience set in your browser, which you can change at any time.

12Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. The website is served over HTTPS, and access to your enquiry is limited to those who need it to assist you. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we work to protect your information at all times.

13Children

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.
